# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P008
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I missed my exams because of a move and no one arranged for me to resit them. Nobody talked to me about university or apprenticeships.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody talked to me about university or apprenticeships.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I had the same social worker for several years and I could trust them. They treated me like a person, not a file.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I waited a few months for counselling and by then I had stopped asking for help. A crisis happened before anyone took it seriously.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I was given a flat with no furniture and nobody to call when the heating broke.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: I was invited to my reviews and my opinion was taken seriously.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: The adults around me kept changing, so I never built a bond with anyone.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Contact with my family was cancelled without any explanation.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make counselling available straight away with no waiting list.
