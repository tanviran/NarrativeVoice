# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P077
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I had two different foster families and my belongings kept getting left behind. I stopped unpacking my bag because I knew I would be moved again.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: It made it hard to trust that anything would last.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: Teachers expected less from me because I was in care. Nobody talked to me about university or apprenticeships.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: The support ended as soon as I turned eighteen.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I could not cook or budget because no one had ever taught me. That said, my personal adviser helped me find a flat and set up my bills before I left.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: My views were written in the file but never seemed to change anything. That said, i had an advocate who made sure my voice was heard.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: My foster carer was the one steady person and I still talk to her.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: She stayed when everything else changed.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: We were placed together as siblings, which was a huge relief.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Moves should be planned and explained, not done overnight.
