# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P042
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: Every time I started to feel at home I was packed up and sent somewhere new. I stopped unpacking my bag because I knew I would be moved again.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: It made it hard to trust that anything would last.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: My teacher kept pushing me and I ended up getting into college.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody asked how I was feeling, they only asked about my behaviour.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I coped on my own and it took a toll on me.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: The support just stopped on my birthday and I had no idea where to turn. I ran into debt because I did not understand the rules.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker asked what I wanted before every decision. Being heard gave me confidence.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: Honestly there was nobody, I felt like I had to manage on my own.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Contact with my family was cancelled without any explanation. That said, i saw my siblings every week and that kept us close.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Moves should be planned and explained, not done overnight.
