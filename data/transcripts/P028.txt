# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P028
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I had five different foster families and my belongings kept getting left behind. That said, i stayed with the same foster family for years and it gave me a real sense of home.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody at school understood what was happening at home, so my grades dropped. That said, the virtual school team arranged a tutor for me and my grades improved.
INTERVIEWER: What was that like for you?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody talked to me about university or apprenticeships.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: They explained decisions to me in a way I could understand.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I could not cook or budget because no one had ever taught me. That said, i had a proper plan for leaving care and I knew what to expect.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: They changed my placement plan because of what I said. Being heard gave me confidence.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: I had no one to turn to when things went wrong. That loneliness is hard to explain.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody helped me rebuild my relationship with my mum. That said, they helped me stay in touch with my family safely.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make sure everyone has at least one adult who stays in their life.
