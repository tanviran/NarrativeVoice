# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P031
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I stayed with the same foster family for years and it gave me a real sense of home.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I missed my exams because of a move and no one arranged for me to resit them.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I hardly ever saw my social worker because they cancelled most visits. It felt like I was just a case number.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I waited nearly two years for counselling and by then I had stopped asking for help. A crisis happened before anyone took it seriously.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Social services supported contact with my gran and it meant a lot.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: Those relationships kept me going.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would give every young person a tutor and a named person at school.
