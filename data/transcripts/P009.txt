# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P009
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I only moved once and the move was explained to me properly beforehand.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I still visit them now.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I got extra help with my exams and it made a big difference.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: Having someone who believed in me changed my attitude to learning.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: She was honest with me even when the news was hard.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I waited a few months for counselling and by then I had stopped asking for help.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: A leaving care team taught me to budget and cook before I moved out.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I felt prepared and a bit less scared.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: They changed my placement plan because of what I said.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: Being heard gave me confidence.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: The adults around me kept changing, so I never built a bond with anyone. That said, a teacher believed in me when nobody else did.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Contact with my family was cancelled without any explanation. That said, they helped me stay in touch with my family safely.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make counselling available straight away with no waiting list.
