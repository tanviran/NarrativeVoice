# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P018
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: Every time I started to feel at home I was packed up and sent somewhere new.
INTERVIEWER: What was that like for you?
PARTICIPANT: It made it hard to trust that anything would last.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I missed my exams because of a move and no one arranged for me to resit them.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker changed every few months and I had to retell my story each time. That said, i had the same social worker for several years and I could trust them.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I felt anxious and low all the time but the service had a long waiting list.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: The support just stopped on my birthday and I had no idea where to turn.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker asked what I wanted before every decision.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: My foster carer was the one steady person and I still talk to her.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: I lost touch with my siblings because we were placed in different towns. I still grieve those lost years.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I still grieve those lost years.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Support should carry on after eighteen.
