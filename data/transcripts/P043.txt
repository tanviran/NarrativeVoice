# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P043
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I was moved six times and I never felt settled anywhere. That said, i only moved once and the move was explained to me properly beforehand.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I wanted to go to college but nobody helped me with the applications.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I had the same social worker for several years and I could trust them.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: They treated me like a person, not a file.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: There was a worker at school I could talk to whenever I needed.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: At eighteen I was moved into a flat on my own and nobody explained how to pay bills.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: I was invited to my reviews and my opinion was taken seriously.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: A mentor from a charity checked in on me every week.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Social services supported contact with my gran and it meant a lot. I am still close to them.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: There should be a gradual handover, not a cliff edge.
