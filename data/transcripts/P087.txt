# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P087
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I changed schools six times and fell behind in almost every subject.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: She was honest with me even when the news was hard. That consistency really mattered to me.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: That consistency really mattered to me.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody asked how I was feeling, they only asked about my behaviour.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I was allowed to stay with my foster carer after eighteen, which made the transition easier.
INTERVIEWER: What was that like for you?
PARTICIPANT: They kept checking in during my first months.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: Decisions about where I lived were made in meetings I was not invited to.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Social services supported contact with my gran and it meant a lot. I am still close to them.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would give every young person a tutor and a named person at school.
