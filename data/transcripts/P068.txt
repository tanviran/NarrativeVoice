# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P068
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: Every time I started to feel at home I was packed up and sent somewhere new.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: They explained decisions to me in a way I could understand.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I was told I was too young or too old for the service, so I was passed around. That said, there was a worker at school I could talk to whenever I needed.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: At eighteen I was moved into a flat on my own and nobody explained how to pay bills.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker asked what I wanted before every decision.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: Being heard gave me confidence.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: People were paid to be there and I could tell it was just a job. I learnt not to rely on anyone.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: I lost touch with my siblings because we were placed in different towns.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make sure everyone has at least one adult who stays in their life.
