# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P065
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: Every time I started to feel at home I was packed up and sent somewhere new. It made it hard to trust that anything would last.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: Teachers expected less from me because I was in care. That said, my teacher kept pushing me and I ended up getting into college.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody talked to me about university or apprenticeships.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I had the same social worker for several years and I could trust them. They treated me like a person, not a file.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I got counselling quickly and it helped me make sense of what had happened.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: The support just stopped on my birthday and I had no idea where to turn.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: A youth worker stayed in my life for years and made the biggest difference.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: I lost touch with my siblings because we were placed in different towns. I still grieve those lost years.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would stop moving young people so often and keep siblings and friends close.
