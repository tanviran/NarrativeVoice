# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P075
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: Moves happened with almost no warning, sometimes on the same day. That said, i only moved once and the move was explained to me properly beforehand.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I changed schools two times and fell behind in almost every subject. That said, the virtual school team arranged a tutor for me and my grades improved.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: I still feel behind compared to people my age.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: They explained decisions to me in a way I could understand.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: The support ended as soon as I turned eighteen.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: Leaving care felt like falling off a cliff with no gradual handover. That said, i had a proper plan for leaving care and I knew what to expect.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: They changed my placement plan because of what I said.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: I had no one to turn to when things went wrong.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Contact with my family was cancelled without any explanation.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Contact should be supported and not cancelled lightly.
