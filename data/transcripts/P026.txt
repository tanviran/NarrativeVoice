# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P026
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I stayed with the same foster family for years and it gave me a real sense of home.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: The virtual school team arranged a tutor for me and my grades improved.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: I am now studying and proud of it.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker visited regularly and remembered the little things about me.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I waited nearly two years for counselling and by then I had stopped asking for help. I coped on my own and it took a toll on me.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I was given a flat with no furniture and nobody to call when the heating broke. That said, a leaving care team taught me to budget and cook before I moved out.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: I had no one to turn to when things went wrong.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: We were placed together as siblings, which was a huge relief.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Support should carry on after eighteen.
