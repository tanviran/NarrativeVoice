# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P050
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I was moved two times and I never felt settled anywhere.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I wanted to go to college but nobody helped me with the applications. I still feel behind compared to people my age.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: She was honest with me even when the news was hard.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: They treated me like a person, not a file.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: The support ended as soon as I turned eighteen.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: The support just stopped on my birthday and I had no idea where to turn. I felt completely alone for the first year.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I ran into debt because I did not understand the rules.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: They changed my placement plan because of what I said.
INTERVIEWER: What was that like for you?
PARTICIPANT: It showed me my opinion mattered.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: A mentor from a charity checked in on me every week.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: I was only allowed to see my brother for an hour a month in an office.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Exams should never be missed because of a move.
