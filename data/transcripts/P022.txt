# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P022
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I stayed with the same foster family for years and it gave me a real sense of home. I still visit them now.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: I got extra help with my exams and it made a big difference.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: My social worker changed every few months and I had to retell my story each time.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I could not cook or budget because no one had ever taught me. That said, my personal adviser helped me find a flat and set up my bills before I left.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: I found out about big decisions after they had been made. I stopped bothering to give my opinion.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: A youth worker stayed in my life for years and made the biggest difference.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: They helped me stay in touch with my family safely. Those relationships kept me going.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make sure we keep the same social worker for years.
