# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P052
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: School was the one place that felt steady for me.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: They talked to the adults and never really to me.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I was told I was too young or too old for the service, so I was passed around. A crisis happened before anyone took it seriously.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I am not sure what to say about that.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody asked me what I thought about my care plan, they just told me the outcome. It felt like my life was being managed, not lived.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: People were paid to be there and I could tell it was just a job.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: We were placed together as siblings, which was a huge relief.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make sure every young person is in their own review meetings.
