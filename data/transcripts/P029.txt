# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P029
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I was moved five times and I never felt settled anywhere. That said, i stayed with the same foster family for years and it gave me a real sense of home.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: School was the one place that felt steady for me.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I had the same social worker for several years and I could trust them.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: The service stayed with me after I left care, which I did not expect.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I had a proper plan for leaving care and I knew what to expect. They kept checking in during my first months.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody asked me what I thought about my care plan, they just told me the outcome.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: A youth worker stayed in my life for years and made the biggest difference.
INTERVIEWER: How did that affect you?
PARTICIPANT: Knowing someone cared gave me strength.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Social services supported contact with my gran and it meant a lot.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I am still close to them.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Young people should have a real say and someone to speak up for them.
