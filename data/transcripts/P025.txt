# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P025
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I was placed miles from my friends and my town, so I lost everyone I knew.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: I never knew who to call when something went wrong.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I got counselling quickly and it helped me make sense of what had happened.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I had a proper plan for leaving care and I knew what to expect.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: They kept checking in during my first months.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: Decisions about where I lived were made in meetings I was not invited to. That said, i was invited to my reviews and my opinion was taken seriously.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: Honestly there was nobody, I felt like I had to manage on my own.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: We were placed together as siblings, which was a huge relief. Those relationships kept me going.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: I would make sure everyone has at least one adult who stays in their life.
