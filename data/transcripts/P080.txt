# SYNTHETIC INTERVIEW P080
INTERVIEWER: Can you tell me about moving between homes or placements while you were in care?
PARTICIPANT: I only moved once and the move was explained to me properly beforehand.
INTERVIEWER: How did being in care affect your education?
PARTICIPANT: The virtual school team arranged a tutor for me and my grades improved. I am now studying and proud of it.
INTERVIEWER: What was your relationship with your social worker like?
PARTICIPANT: She was honest with me even when the news was hard.
INTERVIEWER: Did you get the emotional or mental health support you needed?
PARTICIPANT: I was told I was too young or too old for the service, so I was passed around. I coped on my own and it took a toll on me.
INTERVIEWER: Tell me about leaving care. What was that like?
PARTICIPANT: I was given a flat with no furniture and nobody to call when the heating broke. I felt completely alone for the first year.
INTERVIEWER: How much were you involved in decisions about your life?
PARTICIPANT: Nobody asked me what I thought about my care plan, they just told me the outcome.
INTERVIEWER: Was there anyone who made a real difference to you?
PARTICIPANT: My foster carer was the one steady person and I still talk to her. She stayed when everything else changed.
INTERVIEWER: What about contact with your family or siblings?
PARTICIPANT: Contact with my family was cancelled without any explanation. That said, we were placed together as siblings, which was a huge relief.
INTERVIEWER: Can you say more about that?
PARTICIPANT: I still grieve those lost years.
INTERVIEWER: If you could change one thing about the care system, what would it be?
PARTICIPANT: Support should carry on after eighteen.
